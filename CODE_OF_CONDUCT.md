# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project. Be respectful, give
constructive feedback, and keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and
the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption of
project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and other
spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is
unsafe or persistently disruptive.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub Security
Advisory reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with
appropriate confidentiality.
